Host and edge-node tools (not bundled):
=======================================

The setup and deployment workflows use Bash, Git, OpenSSH tools (ssh, scp,
ssh-copy-id, ssh-keygen), curl, OpenSSL, systemd, Docker Engine, and Docker
Compose v2. These are supplied by the administrator's operating system or
container platform; their versions and license terms depend on that provider.

Manufacturing applications, their MCP servers, container images, and model
artifacts are selected by the deployment inventory and are installed from
their respective repositories or registries. See each application's own
third-party-programs.txt and license notices for its dependencies.
